Method note: only search-result summaries were obtained. Direct fetches of KGK, BDDK, PwC, DergiPark, Bloomberg HT, Alomaliye and GCM failed (host not in session network allowlist), so primary texts were NOT read. Regulatory dates below come from secondary/news summaries and are flagged accordingly.

## 1. Financial institutions: which scores are not meaningful, and what to use instead

### Takeaway
Altman himself states Z-Score was not built for banks, insurers, brokers, investment companies. Leverage/liquidity/EBITDA logic breaks for businesses whose raw material is debt. The bank/insurer-specific substitutes in the literature are ROE, ROA, equity/assets, loan/deposit, P/B vs ROE. I found no authoritative source (regulator/standard-setter) giving numeric "good/bad" ranges for these, so none are given.

### Cited Findings
- Altman says the Z-Score model was not built for financial companies (banks, insurers, brokerages, investment companies, mutual funds); structural reason: different balance-sheet structure, leverage norms, revenue model; high leverage signalling distress is normal for a bank. Practitioner sources suggest regulatory capital measures and sector ratings instead — [search summary of Altman/practitioner sources, e.g. GuruFocus, CFA blog](https://www.gurufocus.com/term/zscore) (secondary; exact Altman quote not verified at primary source).
- Altman Z'' (non-manufacturer / emerging-market variant, 1993/1995; Altman-Hartzell-Peck 1997 for EM) was developed for non-manufacturers and EM firms relying on bank funding; common version: 3.25 + 6.56 X1 + 3.26 X2 + 6.72 X3 + 1.05 X4; zones >2.6 safe, 1.1–2.6 grey, <1.1 distress — [Altman et al. discussion as summarised in IJFS 6(3):70](https://www.doi.org/10.3390/IJFS6030070) (coefficients from a Wikipedia mirror in search; verify against Altman 2018 retrospective). Whether Z'' is intended to exclude financial firms: unverified (one source implies non-manufacturers means non-financial).
- Altman, Iwanicz-Drozdowska, Laitinen, Suvas (2017, J. Int. Fin. Mgmt & Acct 28(2):131–171): general Z''-type model works reasonably for most countries with accuracy about 0.75; country-specific estimation with extra variables can exceed 0.90 — [vLex abstract](https://international.vlex.com/vid/financial-distress-prediction-in-855618057); DOI 10.1111/jifm.12053. Turkey-specific accuracy from this paper: not found.
- Kazakh-bank study applied Altman Z'' to banks with a re-set cut-off and claimed it can detect distressed banks — [IJFS 6(3):70 context](https://www.doi.org/10.3390/IJFS6030070) (one study; contradicts the "do not apply" rule only as an academic re-calibration, not as standard practice).
- BDDK exempted banks and other financing institutions from inflation accounting (see section 2), so bank statements 2025–2026 are in nominal TL; ROE/ROA computed from them are not affected by a monetary-position line (see 2).

### Inferences
- (Analyst convention, derived from the statement logic, not from a cited standard.) F-Score items on current ratio, leverage change, asset turnover, gross margin and CFO/assets have no bank/insurer analogue (no current/non-current split, no gross margin, deposits are operating liabilities, interest is revenue/cost). Z/Z'' inputs (working capital, sales, total liabilities) are similarly undefined. M-Score inputs (receivables index, gross margin index, SG&A index, depreciation index, sales growth) have no bank analogue. Net debt/EBITDA and EV/EBITDA are undefined because interest and deposits are operating items and EV cannot separate operating from financing liabilities. Current ratio does not exist for banks under BDDK format.
- Computable from the nine items (total assets, loans, deposits, equity, NII, net fee income, operating profit, pre-tax profit, net income): ROE = NI / average equity; ROA = NI / average assets; equity/assets; loans/deposits (loan-to-deposit); loans/assets; NII/assets (crude margin proxy); fee income / (NII + fees) (revenue mix); pre-tax profit / assets; P/B = market cap / equity; P/E = market cap / NI. P/B-vs-ROE: justified P/B ~ (ROE − g)/(r − g) (standard Gordon-growth identity; analyst convention). NOT computable from these items: cost/income (needs opex), NPL ratio, capital adequacy ratio (regulatory RWA), net interest margin on earning assets (needs earning assets). Insurers: ROE, equity/assets, P/B, P/E computable; combined ratio and technical result need premium/claims data not in the list.
- Typical interpretation ranges: none stated by an authoritative source found; do not hard-code thresholds.

### Gaps
- No primary regulator/IMF/BIS text retrieved on the bank-ratio set; no authoritative thresholds. Treat the substitute list as analyst convention.
- Insurer, leasing, factoring specifics: no source retrieved on why scores fail beyond the general financial-firm argument. BDDK financial leasing/factoring/financing companies are covered by the same inflation-accounting rule (sec. 2); insurers are regulated by SEDDK, and its inflation-accounting stance for insurers: unverified beyond one summary saying SEDDK joined BDDK in the 2023 year-end exemption.

## 2. TMS 29 / IAS 29 in Turkey

### Takeaway
SPK required listed companies to apply TMS 29 from annual reports for periods ending 31.12.2023. Banks and BDDK-regulated financial institutions were exempted for 2023, deferred for 2025, and (per news summaries) BDDK decided on 18.12.2025 not to apply it at all, so bank statements are nominal while non-financial BIST companies are in current purchasing power. Prior-period comparatives are restated to the current measuring unit, so originally published figures from earlier years are not comparable with later restated ones.

### Cited Findings
- KGK announcement dated 23.11.2023: entities subject to independent audit or voluntarily applying TMS/TFRS apply inflation adjustment in 31.12.2023 financial statements; decision for financial-sector entities left to BDDK and SEDDK — [search summary of KGK announcement](https://www.kgk.gov.tr/Portalv2Uploads/files/Duyurular/v2/Diger/Duyuru17042023.pdf) (URL is a KGK announcement; the 23.11.2023 date comes from the search summary, not read directly; this exact URL name suggests 17.04.2023, so mapping unverified).
- SPK decision (SPK Karar No. 81/1820, 28.12.2023 per my recollection: unverified in this session) required TMS 29 for annual reports for periods ending 31.12.2023; SPK separately gave 10 extra weeks for the first TMS 29 annual reports — [Bloomberg HT summary](https://www.bloomberght.com/spkdan-enflasyon-muhasebesi-aciklamasi-2344793). The decision number is unverified; the content is from the search summary.
- BDDK and SEDDK decided that 31.12.2023 statements need not be adjusted; the 2023-era BDDK decision (dated 11.01.2024 per BDDK summary below) set 1 Jan 2025 as the start for banks, leasing, factoring, financing, savings-financing and asset-management companies — [search summaries incl. Bloomberg HT / Alomaliye](https://www.alomaliye.com/2025/12/23/enflasyon-muhasebesi-uygulamasi-banka-ve-finans-sirketlerinde-kaldirildi/).
- BDDK Board Decision 5.12.2024, No. 11021: inflation accounting not applied for 2025 — [Bloomberg HT / Capital summary](https://www.bloomberght.com/bddk-bankalar-ve-finans-kurumlari-icin-enflasyon-muhasebesi-uygulanmayacak-3764574).
- BDDK Board Decision 18.12.2025, No. 11340 (announced 23.12.2025): banks, leasing, factoring, financing, savings-financing and asset-management companies will not apply inflation accounting; repeals the 11.01.2024 decision; 2025 results prepared outside TMS 29 — [Bloomberg HT](https://www.bloomberght.com/bddk-bankalar-ve-finans-kurumlari-icin-enflasyon-muhasebesi-uygulanmayacak-3764574), [Alomaliye 23.12.2025](https://www.alomaliye.com/2025/12/23/enflasyon-muhasebesi-uygulamasi-banka-ve-finans-sirketlerinde-kaldirildi/). Dates/number come from news; BDDK primary text not read. Another news piece (GCM Yatırım, 26.12.2025) is titled as a "3-year deferral" and concerns tax-side inflation adjustment; its content was not read — unverified. One blog claims the tax return is unadjusted while TFRS statements to investors still use the adjustment; unverified/contradictory with the "no TMS 29 for banks" summary.
- Nothing found of a 2026 change in the BDDK stance (search showed none).
- TMS 29 text: comparatives and any prior-period information are expressed in the current measuring unit at the balance-sheet date; the gain/loss on net monetary position is included in profit or loss and shown separately; some non-monetary items already carried at current amounts (e.g., at market value) are not restated — [KGK TMS 29 guide / TMS 29 text](https://kgk.gov.tr/Portalv2Uploads/files/Duyurular/v2/TDS/TMS_29_Uygulama_Rehberi.pdf) (via search summary).
- Companies with net monetary asset position generally record a monetary loss, hurting net margin (search summary of PwC/KGK material) — [PwC Turkey TMS 29 document](https://pwc.com.tr/tr/Hizmetlerimiz/denetim/finansal-raporlama-muhasebe-danismanligi/tms-29-ve-enflasyon-muhasebesi-finansal-tablolara-etkileri.pdf) (not read directly).
- KGK TMS 29 application guide updated 2025 with sample disclosures for net monetary position gains (losses) following a 26 Oct 2024 Board decision — [Alomaliye 16.01.2025](https://www.alomaliye.com/2025/01/16/enflasyon-muhasebesi-uygulama-rehberi-guncellendi-kgk/).
- KGK also published a high-inflation "presentation currency conversion" announcement (Alomaliye, 14.04.2026) — [link](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/04/14/yuksek-enflasyonlu-sunum-para-birimine-cevrim-resmi-gazetede-yayimlandi/); content not read, unverified.
- An academic study compared ratios of seven BIST food retailers' 2022 statements before and after TMS 29 adjustment — [DergiPark search result](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/3158385); findings not read.

### Inferences
- Comparing "last year's reported" with "this year's reported": last year's original numbers are in the previous year-end TL; this year's report restates the comparative in current TL. A nominal growth calculation mixing originally-published prior values with current-year values overstates growth by roughly the intervening inflation. Use the comparative column inside the latest report, not the old report.
- Comparable across years (if both periods come from the same restated report): same-period ratios whose numerator and denominator are in the same unit (margins, ROA, current ratio, asset turnover, debt/assets). Not comparable: nominal growth of levels from different reports; changes in level items; absolute-TL thresholds. Real growth requires restated comparatives.
- Net monetary position gain/loss is a non-cash, inflation-driven line: it can add to (net monetary liability position) or subtract from (asset position) net income, so net income, ROE, EPS and P/E are partly driven by balance-sheet monetary structure rather than operations. Consider operating profit before monetary line, or net income ex-monetary gain/loss (analyst convention). Equity is also restated, so ROE denominator is inflated, and cash-flow-based items are restated too.
- Banks exempt: their ratios stay nominal, so cross-sector comparisons of ROE/P/E between banks (nominal) and non-banks (real) are inconsistent.

## 3. Empirical evidence in Turkey and emerging markets

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin and heterogeneous. Turkish papers on F-Score mostly study sector subsets and correlations, not return-based out-of-sample tests; Altman Z variants get moderate accuracy (about 0.75 internationally; Turkish snippets 60-75%); Beneish cut-offs vary by study (-1.78, -1.90, -1.35 for "Turkey-modified"), so counts of "manipulators" are not comparable.

### Cited Findings
- Altman et al. (2017), 0.75 average accuracy for the Z''-type general model, above 0.90 with country-specific variables — [vLex](https://international.vlex.com/vid/financial-distress-prediction-in-855618057).
- Abdioglu & Aytekin (2024), Mehmet Akif Ersoy Univ. IIBF Journal 11(4), manufacturing firms on BIST 2017-2024, fixed-effects panel: Altman Z, ROIC, market-to-book, Tobin's Q and Beneish M significantly affect F-Score; higher M (manipulation risk) goes with lower F — [DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/makuiibf/issue/89678/1477251). Not a return test.
- Gulcan & Sakinc (2025): 145 BIST Industrial Index firms 2010-2022, dynamic panel (Arellano-Bond GMM): F-Score positively affects P/E and stock returns; prior-year F-Score correlates positively with market-to-book — [CEEOL](https://www.ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=1340198) (via search summary; effect sizes not read).
- Piotroski & So (2012) and Ng & Shen (2016) cited as showing F-Score pre-sorts Asian firms into winners and losers; no Turkey return study found — [Boğaziçi/other search results](https://core.ac.uk/download/201275613.pdf) (secondary mention; verify).
- Vietnam frontier-market study: M-Score inversely related to excess returns; F-Score positive post-crisis relation — [afj.org.ua / search result](https://afj.org.ua/storage/pdf/1044-zastosuvannya-modeli-z-pokaznika-dlya-prognozuvannya-korporativnih-finansovih-problem-empirichne-doslidzhennya-kompaniy-zareestrovanih-na-fondovomu-rinku-v-etnamu.pdf) (not read; the Z-score article is Vietnamese and the F/M statement was attributed in a search summary, so source mapping is uncertain).
- Beneish on BIST: one study proposed cut-offs -1.90 (original/revised) and -1.35 (modified for Turkey); a 2014-15 study of 412 companies used -1.78 and flagged 55 of 276 firms; panel on 1,256 manufacturing firm-years 2013-2023 found M-Score negatively related to size, age, net margin; a "Beneish TR" sector-wise variant exists — [DergiPark mdbakis](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/mdbakis/issue/64958/853292), [Marmara](https://openaccess.marmara.edu.tr/entities/publication/de7135ad-b6cb-48ea-a01d-b9876637591b), [DergiPark jimeep](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/jimeep/article/1574630). Summaries only; no accuracy rates verified.
- Altman Z-type on Turkish firms: Boğaziçi thesis snippet — about 60% accuracy for distressed, 75% for healthy firms (model unnamed) — [Boğaziçi archive](https://digitalarchive.library.bogazici.edu.tr/bitstreams/0180c98c-15de-44eb-8c2a-06de3f4766e2/download); a comparison of eight distress models in Turkey one year pre-failure found a logistic proposal beat O-Score on overall accuracy — [DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/1125595). Also a study "Application of the Altman Z'' Score Model in Forecasting the Financial Position of BIST Companies" — [DOAJ record](https://library.kab.ac.ug/Record/doaj-art-d1da95340b2545c983ab84ded36dde98/Description); results not read.
- Authors' recommended adjustment (general): re-estimate coefficients and cut-offs locally and add country-specific variables (Altman et al. 2017); sector-wise Beneish cut-offs for Turkey.

### Inferences
- Because most Turkish studies pre-date or ignore TMS 29 restated statements (2023+), their coefficients/cut-offs were estimated on nominal or mixed-basis data; applying them to 2023+ restated data is untested. Index-based items (change in leverage, margin, turnover) are less distorted than level/growth items, but M-Score's sales growth index and F-Score's change-based tests need restated comparatives.

### Gaps
- No Turkey return-test of F-Score, no accuracy rates for Beneish in Turkey or emerging markets verified, no Turkey figure from Altman et al. 2017; Holda (2020) "100% accuracy" claim unconfirmed. Full texts not read.

## 4. Holdings and REITs (GYO)

### Takeaway
GYO and holdings are valued on net asset value (NAD/NAV) discount and P/B rather than margins or leverage scores; investment-property fair-value gains flow through profit and book value, distorting earnings and P/B.

### Cited Findings
- NAD (net asset value) = market value of assets minus liabilities; trading below NAD is called "NAD discount"; a KAP valuation report states the summation (NAD) method is the most appropriate for GYOs — [Fintables](https://fintables.com/arastirma/yazilar/sektor-analiz/nad-nedir), [KAP valuation report](https://kap.org.tr/tr/api/file/download/4028328d98455cf70198c2ccc7a66e4c).
- Seker Yatirim sector analysis: sector market cap / NAD 0.45 (NAD discount 54.8%), P/B 0.47 (book discount 52.6%) (date unclear; one outlier, Koray GMYO, excluded) — [Para Ajansi](https://paraajansi.com.tr/haber/gyo-sektorunde-net-aktif-deger-iskontosu-548e-yukseldi-22231087h).
- YatirimX study using 2025/12 financials and 3 July 2026 prices: highest discounts OZGYO 82.10%, ALGYO 80.22%, EKGYO 59.49% — [YatirimX](https://www.yatirimx.com.tr/piyasa/gyo-hisselerinde-buyuk-iskonto-en-ucuz-hisseler-aciklandi/22007).
- Borsagundem: Emlak Konut GYO NAD 195.1 bn TL at 2025 year-end; trades below NAD — [Borsagundem](https://www.borsagundem.com.tr/portfoy-degeri-en-yuksek-gyo-hisseleri-ve-nad-degerleri).
- Midas: revaluation reports at year-end raise book value and can lower P/B — [Midas](https://www.getmidas.com/midasin-kulaklari/pd-dd-oranina-gore-ucuz-gyo-sirketleri-p-32855).

### Inferences
- (Convention/reasoning, not cited.) Fair-value gains under IAS 40 are non-cash and unrelated to operating cash generation, inflating net income, ROE, and equity; EBITDA/margins exclude or mix them; GYO leverage is asset-backed and Z-Score's working capital logic is irrelevant. Holdings consolidate subsidiaries or equity-account them, so consolidated margins/leverage reflect subsidiaries' structures (including banks); NAV-per-share discount (sum of listed stakes at market + unlisted at estimate - holding net debt) is the standard tool; this was not sourced from a retrieved document. With TMS 29, investment property at fair value is a non-restated item, but equity and other items are restated; effect on P/B is unverified.

### Gaps
- No source on holding-company NAV discount methodology in BIST; no source on IAS 40 / TMS 29 interaction for GYOs; Fintables/Seker pages not read in full.
